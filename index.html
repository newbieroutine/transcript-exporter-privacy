Privacy Policy

YouTube™ Transcript & Subtitle Exporter (Chrome extension)
Effective date: November 21, 2026 (version 0.9.0)

  • Captions are fetched from YouTube and processed on your device. They are never sent to the developer or any other party.
  • Requests to YouTube are sent without your YouTube/Google cookies.
  • AI transcription (Pro, paid) is the only feature that sends video content: only after you click Start, short pieces of the sound of the video playing in your tab are sent to the developer's server on Cloudflare and turned into text there. The text comes back only to you. The developer does not store the audio or the text.
  • Data reaches the developer only when you click something that sends it: a "Report a problem" message you wrote, your email address if you type it in, and, if you use AI transcription, your license key and the audio described above. Nothing is sent before you click.
  • None of these messages include the link, ID or title of the video you are watching, or the transcript.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no sign-in, and no selling of data. AI transcription uses a license key instead of an account; purchases and license keys are handled by Dodo Payments.

1. What the extension does on your device

When you open the extension popup on a YouTube video (or Short), the extension requests that video's caption data from www.youtube.com and turns it into a file on your device (TXT, SRT, VTT or Markdown) or text on your clipboard. Formatting, paragraph building, previews and word counts all happen locally in your browser. If you turn on the "Title & link on top" option, the video title and link are added at the top of the copied text or TXT file; this also happens only on your device.

To get the captions, the extension's script on the YouTube tab sends these requests, and only to www.youtube.com:

  • /youtubei/v1/player — the video's list of caption tracks and basic video details (title, channel name, length and description, used for the file name and the Markdown export);
  • /api/timedtext — the caption text of the track you chose;
  • /youtubei/v1/get_panel — YouTube's transcript panel, used only as a backup when the methods above fail. This request includes the YouTube client version and visitor value that are already present on the YouTube page.

All of these requests are sent without your cookies, so they are not tied to your YouTube or Google account. As with any web request, YouTube can see your IP address. Requests are made only after you open the popup (which loads the caption list and a short preview) or click an export or copy button. Nothing is requested in the background, and the in-page "Transcript" button only opens the popup; it makes no requests itself. The extension does not download any audio or video from YouTube. The only time it uses a video's sound is AI transcription (section 3), which records the sound of the video playing in your tab after you click Start.

When you install or update the extension, YouTube tabs that are already open don't have the extension's scripts yet. At that moment the extension uses Chrome's scripting permission to load its own bundled script and style files (the same ones it adds to every YouTube page) into those open www.youtube.com tabs, so the "Transcript" button works without reloading. This step does not read the page and sends nothing. On first install the extension also opens a welcome page that is part of the extension itself; it loads nothing from the internet.

Optional translation uses Chrome's built-in on-device Translator (and, in backup mode, Chrome's built-in language detector). The transcript text is not sent to the developer. The first time you translate into a language, Chrome itself may download a language pack. AI transcription is different: it does not run on your device (see section 3).

2. "Ask an AI": ChatGPT and Claude

The ChatGPT and Claude buttons copy your prompt, the video title, the video link and the transcript to your clipboard, and then open chatgpt.com or claude.ai in a new tab. The extension sends nothing to those websites. You decide whether to paste the text there; anything you paste is handled under that website's own privacy policy.

3. AI transcription (Pro)

Some videos have no captions at all. For those, AI transcription, a paid Pro feature, can create a transcript from the video's sound. Nothing in this section happens unless you have activated a license key, agreed to the data notice shown before first use ("I agree"), and clicked Start.

How it works. After you click Start, the extension plays the video in your YouTube tab (muted, at the speed you picked) and records its sound from the page while it plays. It does not download the video or its audio from YouTube's servers. The sound is cut into short pieces of up to about 60 seconds of video each. The pieces are sent one at a time to the developer's server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers (ytx-asr.ytx-asr.workers.dev), and are converted to text there by Cloudflare Workers AI (a Whisper speech-recognition model). The text and its timing are sent back only to your browser. When the video ends or you cancel, recording stops and the video's sound, speed and position are put back. Closing the tab or going to another video also stops it. The transcript is made by AI and may contain errors.

What is sent with each piece of audio (over HTTPS, without your cookies):

FieldWhat it contains
AudioThe recorded piece of the video's sound (WebM/Opus, about 32 kbit/s)
License key and activation IDUsed to check that your license is valid and to count the minutes you used
Extension versionFor example 0.9.0

The video link, video ID and title are not sent. Checking how many minutes you have left sends only the license key, activation ID and extension version.

Activating and deactivating a license. When you enter your license key, the extension sends it to the developer's server with a device name such as "Chrome on Windows". The server passes the key and device name to Dodo Payments to activate the key on this device. Clicking "Deactivate on this device" sends the key and activation ID the same way, so the activation is released. The license key and activation ID are kept on your device (see section 7).

What the developer's server keeps. It does not store the audio or the text; they are only passed through to produce the transcript. It keeps:

  • for each license, identified by a SHA-256 hash of the key: the Dodo Payments license, product and customer IDs, and how many minutes you used in the current 30-day period;
  • the result of the last license check, for 10 minutes;
  • request counters per license and per IP address, for about 2 minutes, to limit abuse;
  • short logs that contain only the first 8 characters of the key hash, the audio length and whether the request worked.

As with any web request, Cloudflare can see your IP address. Cloudflare runs the server and the speech recognition; see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Purchase and license key. You buy AI transcription from Dodo Payments. Your payment details, your email address and the license key are handled by Dodo Payments under the Dodo Payments Privacy Policy. The extension never sees your payment details. The developer can see the order in Dodo Payments, including your email address, and uses it only for license and purchase support.

On your device. The finished transcript is kept in the browser's session storage so you can export it after closing the popup. It is deleted when you close Chrome. You can export it in the same formats as captions.

4. Earlier "Pro" interest signal (removed in 0.9.0)

Versions before 0.9.0 had a "Playlist export (Pro)" button. If you clicked it, the extension sent the developer a one-time note through the form service Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com): the event name, extension version, browser language and a random ID generated on your device, plus your email only if you typed it and clicked "Notify me". Version 0.9.0 removed this button and sends nothing of this kind. Emails received this way are used only to tell you about the feature, and you can ask the developer to delete yours (see Contact below).

5. "Report a problem"

The popup has a "Report a problem" link. It opens a small form where you describe what went wrong and, if you want a reply, add your email. Nothing is sent until you click Send. The message goes through the same form service, Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com), to the developer's inbox, with the subject "Transcript Exporter - Feedback".

FieldWhat it contains
Event namefeedback
Extension versionFor example 0.9.0
Browser languageFor example en-US
Your messageWhat you typed (up to 2,000 characters)
Email (optional)Only if you typed one
Error type (only if the popup showed an error)A short internal code, for example no_transcript
Fetch method (only if known)Which way was used to get the text: ios, android or panel for captions, or asr for AI transcription

It also carries the form's access key, the fixed subject line and the sender name "Transcript Exporter". It does not include the video link, video ID, title, page URL, the transcript, your browsing history, your license key or the random ID from section 4. If you want the developer to look at a specific video, you can paste its link into your message yourself.

To avoid accidental repeats, the extension allows one report every 60 seconds and at most 5 in any 24 hours. For this it keeps the times of recently sent reports on your device (see section 7). Reports are used only to find and fix problems, and your email only to reply to you. As with any web request, Web3Forms can see your IP address; the extension does not add it to the message. To have a report or your email deleted, contact the developer.

6. Uninstall feedback page

When you remove the extension, Chrome opens this web page: https://newbieroutine.github.io/transcript-exporter-privacy/uninstall.html?v=<version>. The only thing added to the address is the extension's version number (for example ?v=0.9.0). The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, next to this policy. It loads nothing from other sites and has no analytics; like any website host, GitHub can see your IP address when the page loads.

The page has a short optional form: why you removed the extension (checkboxes), an optional comment and an optional email. Nothing is sent unless you click Send. If you do, your answers and the version number go through Web3Forms to the developer's inbox, with the subject "Transcript Exporter - Uninstall". You can also just close the page.

8. What we do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels or advertising. The extension sends nothing in the background. The only data it sends to the developer is described in sections 3, 4 and 5, and only after you click to send it (for AI transcription, after you click Start).
  • No user accounts or sign-in. AI transcription uses a license key (section 3).
  • We do not collect your browsing history, the videos you watch, or the transcripts you export. Audio and text from AI transcription pass through the developer's server only to make the transcript and are not stored.
  • We do not sell or rent data. We share it only with the service providers named in this policy (Web3Forms, Cloudflare and Dodo Payments), only to provide the features described here, and we do not use it for any purpose unrelated to the extension.
  • No remote code: all of the extension's code is included in the package published on the Chrome Web Store.

9. Children's privacy

The extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us an email address or a message through the Pro form, "Report a problem" or the uninstall page, please contact us and we will delete it.
